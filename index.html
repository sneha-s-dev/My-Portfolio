Business Communication (SB1022) Hub

Course Overview: 5 Core Modules, Submissions, Classroom Activities & Video Takeaways.

5 Course Modules

  1. Module 1: Communication, Types & Barriers
  2. Module 2: Types of Non-Verbal Communication
  3. Module 3: Business Writing (Emails, Messages & Memos)
  4. Module 4: Business Reports & Data Visuals
  5. Module 5: Debates, Discussions & Presentations

Professional Vocabulary & Learnings

  • Audience First: Tailoring message structure to reader needs before writing.
  • Rule of Three: Presenting key points in threes for maximum retention.
  • Digital Body Language: Formatting cues that signal professionalism online.
  • F-Pattern Scanning: Structuring web copy with bold headings for quick scanning.
  • 7 Cs of Communication: Clear, Concise, Concrete, Correct, Coherent, Complete, Courteous.
